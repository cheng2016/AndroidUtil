# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AndroidUtil a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while challenging ideas with evidence.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and learn from feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project and its community.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `mitnick.cheng@outlook.com`. Maintainers will investigate fairly and may remove content or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
